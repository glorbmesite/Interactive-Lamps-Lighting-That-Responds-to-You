<h3 data-start="618" data-end="658">How Do Interactive Lamps Work?</h3>
<p data-start="659" data-end="1113">Interactive lamps rely on LED technology, sensors, and smart controls. Touch-sensitive models let you adjust settings with a simple tap. Advanced models connect to apps or smart home systems, allowing full customization, scheduling, and synchronization with other smart devices.</p>
